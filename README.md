# Project Management & Company Management Course

A comprehensive course covering the fundamentals and advanced practices of **Project Management** and **Company (Business) Management**.

## Overview

This course provides a practical and theoretical foundation for managing projects effectively and leading organizations successfully. It combines proven methodologies (Agile, Waterfall, hybrid approaches) with essential company management principles such as strategy, operations, leadership, finance, and organizational behavior.

Whether you are an aspiring project manager, team lead, entrepreneur, or business professional, this course equips you with the skills to deliver projects on time, within budget, and aligned with organizational goals.

## Learning Objectives

By the end of this course, you will be able to:

- Apply core project management frameworks (PMBOK, PRINCE2, Agile/Scrum)
- Plan, execute, monitor, and close projects successfully
- Manage scope, schedule, cost, quality, risk, and stakeholders
- Understand company structure, strategy, and governance
- Lead teams and manage organizational change
- Align project outcomes with business strategy
- Make informed decisions using financial and operational metrics
- Foster a culture of continuous improvement and accountability

## Course Structure

### Module 1: Foundations of Project Management
- What is a project vs. operations
- Project lifecycle and process groups
- Role of the Project Manager
- Key success factors and common failure reasons

### Module 2: Project Planning & Initiation
- Defining project charter and scope
- Work Breakdown Structure (WBS)
- Scheduling techniques (Gantt, Critical Path Method)
- Resource and cost estimation
- Risk identification and response planning

### Module 3: Execution, Monitoring & Control
- Team leadership and communication
- Agile practices (Scrum, Kanban)
- Performance measurement (Earned Value Management)
- Change control and issue management
- Quality assurance and control

### Module 4: Project Closure & Lessons Learned
- Handover and acceptance
- Post-project evaluation
- Knowledge management

### Module 5: Company Management Fundamentals
- Organizational structures and culture
- Strategic management and business models
- Leadership styles and decision-making
- Operations and process management
- Financial management basics for managers

### Module 6: Integrating Project & Company Management
- Portfolio and program management
- Aligning projects with corporate strategy
- Governance, PMO, and organizational project management maturity
- Change management and organizational transformation
- Sustainability and ethical considerations
